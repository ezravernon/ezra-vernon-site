The fine print

Accessibility

I want everyone to be able to read and use this site, whatever device or assistive technology they use.

Last updated November 14, 2026

What I’m aiming for

The site is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What’s in place

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Headings, landmarks, lists and tables are marked up so screen readers can navigate by them.
  • Photos have text descriptions. Purely decorative images are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Text and controls meet AA colour contrast, and nothing relies on colour alone.
  • Pages reflow on small screens and when zoomed, without sideways scrolling.
  • Animations are switched off when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The charts on the Training log have the same numbers available as a table and a list.

Known limits

  • I check the site with automated accessibility tests and by keyboard. It has not had a professional audit or testing with every screen reader.
  • The charts on the Training log are drawn as graphics. Each bar and slice has a text label, but the table and list are the easier way to read the data.
  • Some text sits over photographs. It has a dark overlay behind it, but contrast can vary a little with the photo.
  • Sites I link to, such as Instagram, LinkedIn and Strava, are outside my control.

Tell me about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use or doesn’t work with your setup, please let me know through the Socials page, and tell me the page and what went wrong. I will do my best to fix it.
