Things I’ve been thinking about.

Nov 19, 2026

Should we make an effort for the normal life?

I’ve been thinking about the idea of a normal life.

My definition of this seems to contain four parts; A stable job, renting or working towards a home, a daily routine, long-term relationships, and raising children.

With all of the instability and greed globally, it is hard to act like the world is heading towards a good place. It feels like a massive global problem pops up every week.

Wars, the climate crisis, human genocides, global widespread hacking, fears about AI and agentic agents, the overall decline of human connection in everyday life, and declining birth rates. All of those, and I don’t even scratch the surface. All of these feel like huge societal problems that leave the general public feeling hopeless, and feeling like we are in a place where no one can do anything, always blaming it on someone else.

With that, the question remains: should we continue to strive for a normal life when all of these giant problems exist?

I believe these problems can, and will, result in the extinction or dramatic change of the human race as we know it. The timeline seems to depend on whether we can solve the problems in time. Given this apocalyptic view and urgency that comes from it, the answer to this question should be simple: no.

Unfortunately, it is not as simple as that. I think that there is an alternative outcome that could happen. The most reliable and common outcome is that the world will change in a way that will make our current world seem distant and irregular. I think that we have a chance to save it and work together to create something that everyone can enjoy, but for now nothing is certain.

Please take this entry with a grain of salt, and nothing that I say is determined; it is my own opinion, and just that.

Nov 19, 2026

Life

This entry ironically comes from an Instagram reel and a quote.
“One life, just one. Why aren’t we running like we are on fire towards our wildest dreams?”

This quote comes from Dean Grazoisi and is a great reminder of how people take life for granted.

I recently had someone, not necessarily close to me personally, but very close to my community, pass away. Someone who was not afraid of expressing his thoughts, someone who fought for his friends, stuck up for the right thing, and made the world a better place. Seeing the abrupt disappearance and death of someone like this reminds me of how lucky everyone is to be in this world.

We live in a world where basic needs have a practically nonexistent barrier to entry, where work is something that we don’t understand, and enjoying life is a circular choice. We take life as something that is determined, something that can be accurately predicted, even when it is the absolute opposite of that.

We have one life, one that ends as quickly as it starts, and one that can be taken away far before we turn 80. Take care of it, value it, and chase it.

Ezra Vernon · Live in the balance between two extremes.
