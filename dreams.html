02

Mount Drew

2,608 m · Squamish, BC

The notorious peak located in Squamish, BC.

The highest peak, standing at a wild 2,608 metres, it has always been a dream of mine to summit. It is one of the biggest glaciers in this region, making it very dangerous to climb and requiring mountaineering experience.

The whole range spans 1,100,000 acres and has many peaks inside the area.

03

Mount Tantalus

2,603 m · Tantalus Range

The highest summit of the Tantalus Range, the wall of glaciated peaks you see across the Squamish Valley from the Sea to Sky Highway. The Squamish people call the range Tsewílx’.

First climbed in 1911, it is a serious glacier-and-ridge objective: crevasses, bergschrunds and an exposed summit ridge, usually approached by helicopter or a long climb up from near sea level.
